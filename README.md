# Pookpit | Ad creative production

## Overview

A collection of five ad videos created around Pookpit, showing my ability to develop an idea and turn it into a finished creative.

## My role

Original concepts, scripts and full creative production.

## What this work demonstrates

- Developing ad ideas around a brand and its product concept.
- Writing the scripts and producing the final videos.
- Presenting multiple creative executions in a single watchable portfolio.

## Scope and limits

This is creative-production work. It is not a claim of a commissioned brand engagement, paid media buying, campaign management or measured advertising results. No ad spend, reach, click-through rate, conversions or return on ad spend is claimed.
